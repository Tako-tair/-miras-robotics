03INNOVATION PROJECT

"Air Over Almaty"

Addressing urban winter smog and PM2.5 particulate pollution through an autonomous dual-system. The robot measures local air conditions, decides filtration intensity, and triggers active cleaning cycles.

The problem

Why is Almaty's winter air so dirty?

The city lies in a basin at the foot of the Ile Alatau mountains. On calm winter days a layer of warm air settles on top like a lid, and the smoke has nowhere to go.

  • Basin & inversionWeak winds and a warm 'lid' hold cold, polluted air over the city.
  • Coal heatingPower plants and private homes burn coal all winter long.
  • TrafficHundreds of thousands of cars add exhaust fumes every day.

BIOGLOW™

Why smog is a BIOGLOW problem

The BIOGLOW challenge asks teams to find a problem that puts biodiversity at risk. Smog does not only hurt people: the whole living city breathes it.

Trees & plants

Fine dust settles on leaves, clogs their tiny pores and blocks sunlight, so plants grow weaker.

Bees & pollinators

Polluted air breaks down the scent of flowers, so bees and other pollinators find them harder.

Birds & lichens

Birds breathe the same particles we do. Lichens, living indicators of clean air, disappear from smoggy streets.

How small is PM2.5?

About 30 times thinner than a human hair: small enough to reach deep into the lungs and even the bloodstream.

The safe limit
15µg/m³

Almaty is the homeland of the apple

The wild Sievers apple, ancestor of the apples we eat, grows in the Ile Alatau foothills. Clean air helps protect this unique nature.

Our solution
01

Measured

PMS5003 sensor detects real-time PM2.5 concentration levels in winter urban conditions.

02

Decided

ESP32 microcontroller evaluates particle density and calculates required airflow speed.

03

Purified

Radial fan draws air through GL-2118 particle filter, trapping airborne pollutants.

04

Championship

Showcased at FLL tournaments with full robot runs and project pitch.

Engineering design process
  1. 1Identify

    Smog in Almaty harms people, plants, pollinators and birds. We studied PM2.5 and where it comes from.

  2. 2Design

    We compared different ways to clean air and chose a fan with a particle filter: simple, affordable and works in frosty winter.

  3. 3Create

    We built a prototype: ESP32, PMS5003 sensor, radial fan and GL-2118 filter in a 3D-printed housing.

  4. 4Iterate

    We tested sensor accuracy and the filtration system and improved the prototype after every test.

  5. 5Communicate

    We shared the project at the Almaty Scrimmage, at our school stand and on Instagram.
